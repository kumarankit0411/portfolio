## Overview

Architected and maintained a high-performance Vanilla JS engine used by Fortune 500 retailers to execute marketing campaigns. The engine handles dynamic product recommendations, social proofing, badging systems, and real-time analytics — all while maintaining zero-latency execution during high-revenue sales events.

## Key Features

- Dynamic product recommendation injection
- Social proof notifications (recent purchases, low stock alerts)
- Badging system for promotions and discounts
- Real-time analytics tracking
- Support for complex campaign types

## Performance

The engine was optimized to execute within browser's requestAnimationFrame cycle, ensuring no layout thrashing or jank. Achieved zero-latency execution during Black Friday and Cyber Monday events for Nike and Reebok.

## Impact

Drove millions in revenue through optimized campaign delivery. The engine serves as the primary campaign execution layer for multiple Tier-1 retailers across North America.
