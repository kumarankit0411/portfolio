## Overview

Biker Bazaar is a two-sided marketplace I founded and built for India's motorcycle community — a place to buy and sell used motorcycles, helmets, boots, and riding gear. I'm the founder, CEO, and sole engineer; the product is live at [bikerbazaar.in](https://bikerbazaar.in).

## Why I'm Building It

India has one of the largest motorcycle markets in the world, but buying and selling used gear is fragmented across WhatsApp groups, social media posts, and scattered forums. Biker Bazaar gives riders a single, searchable marketplace with structured listings, verified sellers, and a simple payment and monetization loop.

## What's Shipped

- **Marketplace core**: searchable listings with category, brand, and size filters for bikes, helmets, boots, and riding gear
- **Selling flow**: an in-depth listing form that turns inputs into clean, structured listings
- **Monetization**: Razorpay payments with a tiered premium model (Basic, Pro, Max) — featured slots, listing limits, and buyer-facing phone unlock
- **Editorial engine**: an SEO blog with India-specific buying guides (helmet safety ratings, used-gear checklists, valuation guides) driving organic traffic
- **Infrastructure**: Supabase-backed auth, database, and storage; sitemap/robots; deployed on Vercel

## What's Next

Growing supply and demand, premium-fleet retention, and building out the marketplace features riders ask for most. This is my full-time gig — the roadmap evolves as the community tells me what it needs.

## Stack

- **Next.js + TypeScript** — the entire product, storefront to admin
- **Supabase** — Postgres, auth, storage
- **Razorpay** — payments and subscriptions
- **Vercel** — hosting, deployment, analytics
